Add “Chess move” Shortcut

Your personal chess link. It keeps your game separate from everyone else's.

Your Gemini key:

Build the Shortcut yourself
  1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, and name it Chess move.
  2. Add Get Latest Photos: 1 photo, Include Screenshots off.
  3. Add Convert Image → JPEG.
  4. Add Resize Image → width 1400.
  5. Add Get Contents of URL and paste your chess link. Expand it and set Method to POST. Only if you added your own key: under Headers, add x-gemini-key with your key as the value. Set Request Body to File → Resized Image.
  6. Add Get Dictionary Value → key say in Contents of URL.
  7. Add Speak Text → Dictionary Value.
4

Make it hands-free

  1. In the Meta AI app, turn on automatic import of photos from your glasses to the Photos app.
  2. On your iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap → Chess move. The Action Button or “Hey Siri, chess move” work too.

During a game: when it's your move, look at the board and say “Hey Meta, take a photo”. Wait a few seconds for it to import, then double-tap the back of your phone. Take a photo after your own move as well, so the app keeps track of the game.
