Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The chess engine (Stockfish) is free and runs on this site. Reading the board uses Google's Gemini AI: you can use this site's shared key for a number of free scans a day, or add your own free Gemini key for unlimited scans.

Which glasses and phones work?

Any Meta AI glasses that take photos (Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta) paired with an iPhone. The hands-free part uses an Apple Shortcut. On Android, or without glasses, open the live board and use your phone's camera instead.

How accurate is it?

It's best when you start from the beginning of a game and take a photo after every move. Each new photo then only has to show which legal move was played, so small misreads get corrected automatically. If something looks wrong, you can fix the position on the live board.

What happens to my photos?

Each photo is sent to Google's Gemini to read the board and isn't stored by this site. If you add your own key, it stays on your phone and in your Shortcut. Google may use images sent on its free tier to improve its products.

Can I use it in tournaments?

No. Using engine help in rated, online or tournament games is cheating. Chess Glasses is for practice, studying positions and casual games where everyone knows.
