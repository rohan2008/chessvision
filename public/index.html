Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The chess engine (Stockfish) is free. Reading the board uses Google's Gemini AI: you get a number of free scans a day with this site's shared key, or unlimited scans if you add your own free Gemini key in Settings.

Which cameras work?

Any camera your browser can use: a phone's back or front camera, a laptop webcam, or a USB camera. Pick it from the list on the Play page. Meta glasses work by taking photos (iPhone Shortcut) or through a video call you share from a computer.

How do I get the best results?

Hold or mount the camera above the board, so all 64 squares are in view and not too steep an angle. Start scanning from the beginning of a game, and scan after every move. Each new scan then only has to show which legal move was played, so small misreads get corrected automatically.

What happens to my pictures?

Each picture is sent to Google's Gemini to read the board and isn't stored by this site. If you add your own key, it stays on your device. Google may use images sent on its free tier to improve its products.

Can I use it in tournaments?

No. Using engine help in rated, online or tournament games is cheating. Chess Vision is for practice, studying positions and casual games where everyone knows.
